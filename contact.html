Common Inquiries

How soon can you start working on my project?

I typically begin work within 1-3 business days after our initial consultation and agreement. The exact timeline depends on the scope of work and current project load.

What information do you need from me to get started?

For a website, I'll need your business details, branding (logo/colors if available), and content or page ideas. For SEO or ads, I'll need access to your website/analytics and details about your target audience. We'll cover all this during our initial consultation.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes, I work with clients worldwide. All communication, project updates, and deliverables are handled remotely via email, WhatsApp, or video calls, so location is never a barrier.

How do you measure the success of your work?

Success is measured through key metrics like keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, site speed, conversion rate, and cost-per-click on ad campaigns. I provide regular reports detailing these metrics.

Can you redesign my existing website instead of building new?

Yes, absolutely. I can audit your current site, fix design and performance issues, and improve SEO without starting over — whatever makes the most sense for your budget and timeline.

Do you offer a complete package (website + SEO + ads)?

Yes! I offer a Full Growth Package that combines website development, complete SEO (on-page, off-page, technical), and Meta/Google Ads management — all handled by me directly for a consistent strategy.
